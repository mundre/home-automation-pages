Privacy policy

Your home data stays yours.

Effective September 5, 2026

Overview

Home Automation is a personal, non-commercial application operated for the sole use of its owner. It uses Google Device Access only after the owner explicitly authorizes access to their Google Nest devices.

Information accessed

The application may access device names and states, thermostat temperature and humidity readings, and doorbell or camera events and associated media made available by Google Device Access.

How information is used

Information is used only to display smart-home status, maintain a personal event history, select representative event images, and support personal home automations.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored on private systems controlled by the owner. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties, except for service providers required to operate the authorized Google APIs.

Retention and deletion

Raw media may be deleted automatically after representative images are selected. The owner controls retention and may delete stored application data at any time.

Access and revocation

The owner can revoke the application's Google Account access at any time from their Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents future access through Google Device Access.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
