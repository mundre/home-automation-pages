H Home Automation

Personal smart-home utility

A private view of the home.

Home Automation is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to access and organize information from authorized smart-home devices.

Purpose

The application helps its owner review doorbell activity and environmental readings from devices connected through Google Device Access.

Private by design

Device information is processed for personal use and stored on systems controlled by the owner. This site contains no device data.

Limited access

The application is not offered as a public service and does not sell access, advertising, or personal information.

Privacy policy Personal use only
